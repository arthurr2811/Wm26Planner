A simple, clean planner for the 2026 FIFA World Cup without the fluff. Shows live match results and supports three modes:

Mode 1 – Full Simulation: Predict every match from the group stage onwards
Mode 2 – Continue from now: Pick up from the current tournament state and tip your way through the remaining matches
Mode 3 – Live Standings: Just follow the real tournament, group tables, results, and knockout bracket

Teams are automatically seeded into the knockout stage based on group results.
